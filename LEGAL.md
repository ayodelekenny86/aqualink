Last updated: October 15, 2026

Our refund policy applies to all bookings made through the Aqualink application.

### 1. Eligibility for Refunds
Refunds may be issued in the following circumstances:
- Service not delivered as booked (wrong address, wrong time, etc.)
- Water quality issues verified by our quality team
- Duplicate payments for the same booking
- Technical errors in our system that resulted in incorrect charges

### 2. Non-Refundable Situations
Refunds will not be provided for:
- Change of mind after service completion
- Minor variations in delivery time (±15 minutes)
- Water temperature variations within acceptable range
- Issues caused by customer-provided incorrect address or contact information
- Cancellations made less than 2 hours before scheduled delivery time

### 3. Refund Process
To request a refund:
1. Contact our support team through the app or at support@aqualink.com
2. Provide your booking reference number and details of the issue
3. Our team will investigate the claim within 2 business days
4. If approved, refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 5-10 business days

### 4. Partial Refunds
In cases where only part of the service was unsatisfactory, we may issue a partial refund proportional to the issue.

### 5. Refund Method
All refunds will be issued to the original payment method used for the booking. We do not offer store credit or cash refunds unless required by law.
